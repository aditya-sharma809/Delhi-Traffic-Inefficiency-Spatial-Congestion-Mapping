# EXECUTIVE TRAFFIC ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS REPORT
**Project:** Delhi Traffic Inefficiency & Spatial Congestion Mapping  
**Author:** Aditya Sharma  

## 1. Executive Summary & Key Metrics
This report synthesizes granular traffic monitoring data across 2,976 time intervals. The primary objective is to evaluate road usage efficiency, identify spatial-temporal bottlenecks, and evaluate vehicle composition dynamics.

---

## 2. Business Questions & Visual Intelligence Findings

### BQ1: Temporal Bottlenecks & Peak Congestion Windows
* **Key Finding:** Sustained congestion peaks during Evening Rush (16:00 - 19:00) hitting maximum PCU Scores of ~246. Morning Rush (07:00 - 10:00) maintains ~233 PCU.
* **Actionable Policy:** Deploy dynamic adaptive traffic signals at 06:30 and 15:30.

### BQ2: Vehicular Composition & Road Inefficiency
* **Key Finding:** Private cars occupy 60.1% of all total vehicle volume, whereas buses and trucks represent 13.4% each, and two-wheelers hold 13.1%.
* **Actionable Policy:** Promote HOV (High Occupancy Vehicle) lanes to curb solo-car transit load.

### BQ3: Day Part Infrastructure Demand
* **Key Finding:** Evening (1.20) and Morning (1.18) exhibit the highest Congestion Index relative to the monthly average baseline. Night index drops to 0.58.
* **Actionable Policy:** Mandate heavy commercial logistics movement exclusively within the Night Window (22:00 - 05:00).

### BQ4: Traffic Attribute Correlation Analysis
* **Key Finding:** PCU Score strongly correlates with Bus Count (r = 0.87) and Car Count (r = 0.86). Truck Count shows strong negative correlation with passenger vehicles, indicating speed/space throttling.
* **Actionable Policy:** Enforce peak-hour heavy vehicle entry bans on critical urban arterial roads.
